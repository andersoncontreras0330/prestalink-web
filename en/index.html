PrestaLink

Your loans, in order

PrestaLink is a ledger for loans between people who already know each other. We are not the lender and we don't find lenders: we don't put up the money, we don't charge interest and we don't decide who gets a loan. What we do is make sure the terms are written down before the money changes hands, and that the app, not each person's memory, keeps track of what is owed.
