How to install it

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks for permission to install apps from your browser or from Files, allow it.
  3. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns that the app doesn't come from Google Play, tap More details › Install anyway.

You can turn off the permission from step 2 again afterwards.

If you already had PrestaLink from Google Play

Uninstall it first: Android doesn't let one replace the other. Your data is in your account, not on the phone, so when you sign in again everything is still there.
