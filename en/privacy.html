Privacy Policy

We keep what is needed for a loan to be recorded and verifiable. We don't sell data. Everyone sees only their own.

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What data we collect

Identity: name, date of birth, ID type and number, and the photos of the ID you upload.

Contact and location: email, phone, address, city, and country.

Lender data: their deposit method and the details of the account where they receive money.

Activity: requests, loans, installments, and recorded payments.

We do not collect data from your device for advertising purposes, and we do not use third-party trackers.

2. What we use it for

So that a lender can know who they are lending to, which is why documents are requested.

To keep track of what is owed and what has been paid.

To contact you about your account or your loans.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Who sees what

The lender you send a request to sees your identity and contact details and the photos of your ID. That is what allows them to decide whether to lend to you.

The borrower sees the lender's name, code, city and the terms they publish. Once the lender approves a loan for them, the borrower also sees the lender's deposit methods with the details the lender entered for each one (for example, bank, account number and holder), so they know how to receive the money and how to pay. They do not see the lender's portfolio or other clients.

No user sees another user's loans, clients, or documents. This does not depend on the application but on the database rules, which reject any query for other people's data.

4. Who it is shared with

With Supabase, which hosts the database and files on our behalf and under our instructions.

If you are a lender and subscribe: with Apple or Google (or the external processor you pay with), which charge the subscription, and with RevenueCat, which confirms its status to us. They receive your user identifier and the purchase details, but not the details of your loans or your clients.

With Sentry, which receives the app's error reports so we can fix them: what failed, on which screen, your phone's model and operating system, the app version and your user identifier. It does not receive your name, your email, your documents or the details of your loans.

With an authority, when a valid legal order requires us to.

With no one else.

5. The photos of your ID

They are stored in private storage, in a separate folder for each user. They cannot be accessed through a public link.

Only the lender you sent a request to can see them. They are not used for anything else.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists, and afterward for the time required by applicable legal or accounting obligations.

If you delete your account, we erase your personal data, the photos of your ID, your email, and your access. If you had loans, the other party keeps that history with your name marked "account deleted", because it is also their record of what they lent or paid.

While you have loans in progress, the account cannot be deleted: their history is the proof of what is owed.

7. Your rights

Access your data and correct it: you can edit it yourself under More › Personal details.

Ask us to delete your account and your data. If you have active loans, they must be closed first: what is owed cannot be erased unilaterally because it affects the other party.

Object to processing or request a copy of your data by writing to prestalink.soporte@gmail.com

8. Security

All traffic between the application and the server is encrypted.

Access to data is controlled by the database row by row, not by the application: even if someone queried the server outside the app, they would only get their own data.

Passwords are stored encrypted and no one, including us, can read them.

9. Minors

PrestaLink is not directed at people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we detect an account belonging to a minor, we close it and delete its data.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will notify you in the application.

Data controller and contact: prestalink.soporte@gmail.com
