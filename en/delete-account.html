Delete your account

You can delete your PrestaLink account and your data at any time.

From the app

  1. Open PrestaLink and sign in.
  2. Go to More › Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

If you have loans in progress (proposed, awaiting disbursement or active), they must be finished first: their history is the record of what is owed.

If you can't sign in anymore

Write to prestalink.soporte@gmail.com from your account's email, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days at most and let you know.

What gets deleted

  • Your personal data: ID, phone, address and deposit details.
  • The photos of your ID.
  • Your email and your access: you won't be able to sign in with this account again.
  • Your contacts and your notifications.
  • Your code and your lending terms: no one will be able to find you to request loans.

What remains

  • If you had loans, the other person keeps that history with your name, marked "account deleted". Nothing else.
  • Your pending requests are closed.

You have an active subscription. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it: the store would keep charging you. Cancel it first from "Subscription".
